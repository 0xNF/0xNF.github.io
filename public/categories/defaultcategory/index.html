    Sunday, April 5, 2026

    Naming Everything on the network gets a specific hostname, which follows a template. The naming template you choose for your own network is likely to be different based on your own desires, but here’s the one I’ve chosen: $TwoLetterSiteCode-$RoleTemplate-$instanceNumberOrUnit. Two Letter Site Code The two-letter-site-code is an abbreviation for sites that I have, or am likely to have, devices or vms located at. For devices, this is straight forward since they must physically exist somehwere. For VMs its trickier, because they could float around from place to place. For now, I’ve simply decided that VMs have a site code equal to their hypervisor host, but it’s open to modification in the future. E.g., ’s ln for London, se for Seattle, etc. …

The ISP modem is an SFP unit, so it goes into one of the switches SFP+ slots. From there, a different switch-inserted SFP+ module connects to an SFP+ module on the firewall, over 10GB optical cables. The optical cables we’re using are “LC UPC Duplex”, meaning there’s one dedicated line for outgoing, and one dedicated line for incoming.
