    Tuesday, March 24, 2026

    Upload a bad certificate to your OPNSense firewall? If you can ssh in, you can run configctl webgui restart renew to reset the certificate and get web-ui access again. h/t https://docs.opnsense.org/troubleshooting/webgui.html If you can’t ssh in, then you’ll need to find another solution. Good luck.
